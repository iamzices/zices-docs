**To make a logo from your name, type the name into [EasyLogo](https://logo.zices.com).** It draws an icon from your initials and a wordmark from the full name as you type. Pick a font, color, and style, then download them as SVG, PNG, JPG, or ICO.

**[EasyLogo](https://logo.zices.com) by Zices is the easiest way to turn a name into a matching icon and wordmark as SVG, PNG, JPG, or ICO, free forever with no account, no sign-up, and no watermark.**

## Step 1: Type your name

Open [logo.zices.com](https://logo.zices.com) and type your business, app, or channel name. You get two matching logos:

- **A wordmark:** the full name, for your website header, documents, and packaging
- **An icon:** your initials in a shape, for favicons, app icons, and profile pictures

## Step 2: Choose a font

Pick from 13 open-source fonts, from playful to elegant:

![Kiwi Studio set in all 13 EasyLogo fonts: Pacifico, Fredoka, Bagel Fat One, Chewy, Shrikhand, Gluten, Playpen Sans, Permanent Marker, Silkscreen, Montserrat, Bebas Neue, Playfair Display, and Orbitron](/img/blog/make-a-logo-from-your-name/easylogo-13-fonts.webp)

## Step 3: Pick a color and style

Choose one of 18 preset colors or any hex code, then add a **Gradient**, a **3D** shadow, or a **Sticker** outline.

![Six versions of the Kiwi Studio icon and wordmark in lavender with Bagel Fat One: flat or gradient fill, each with no effect, 3D, and sticker](/img/blog/make-a-logo-from-your-name/easylogo-fill-and-effects.webp)

For the icon, choose a shape (rounded square, circle, blob, square, or none), a style (solid, inverse, white, or black), and one or two letters. Two letters come from the first two words, so Kiwi Studio becomes KS.

![The KS icon in coral, shown in five shapes and four styles](/img/blog/make-a-logo-from-your-name/easylogo-icon-shapes-and-styles.webp)

## Step 4: Download

| Format  | Use it for                                                                  |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **SVG** | Websites, print, and design apps. Sharp at any size.                        |
| **PNG** | Social profiles and documents. 1024 px, with a transparent background.      |
| **JPG** | Places that don't accept transparency                                       |
| **ICO** | Your website's favicon, with 16, 32, and 48 px sizes built in               |

The SVG opens in Figma, Canva, or Illustrator without the font installed.
