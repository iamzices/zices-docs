## Why convert to SVG?

PNG, JPG, and WebP images are made of pixels, so they turn blurry or jagged when you enlarge them. An SVG stores shapes instead, so it stays sharp at any size, from a favicon to a billboard, and you can change its colors in any vector editor.

Some converters just put the original pixels inside an `.svg` file. EasySVG redraws the image as real vector paths.

## Step 3: Adjust the settings if needed

The defaults suit most logos. If something looks off:

- **Colours:** set the exact number of colors if two similar colors were merged
- **Detail:** slide toward Simpler to remove specks, or Finer to keep small details
- **Corners:** slide toward Sharp for geometric logos, or Rounded for hand-drawn art
- **Remove background:** make a white or solid background transparent

## Step 4: Download

Click **Download** to save the SVG, or the copy button to copy its code. It opens in Illustrator, Inkscape, Figma, and any browser.

## What converts well

Tracing works best on flat colors: logos, icons, clip art, stickers, illustrations, and line art. Photos and soft gradients don't convert well, so keep those as JPG or WebP.
